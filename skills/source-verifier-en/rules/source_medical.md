# Healthcare Information Special Rules

Healthcare is a high-risk domain. Must use high-quality sources and avoid packaging weak evidence as diagnostic or treatment recommendations.

## Best Sources

```text
Clinical guidelines
Government health agencies
Drug regulatory agencies
Medical associations
Systematic reviews
Meta-analysis
Peer-reviewed medical papers
Large hospital educational pages
Drug prescribing information
```

## Weak Sources

```text
Influencer health bloggers
Supplement websites
Patient personal stories
Forum experiences
Marketing advertorials
Unqualified medical blogs
Short video comments
```

## Must Check

```text
Publication or update date
Whether it is a guideline or review
Whether applicable to the user's region
Whether commercial interests exist
Whether intended for adults, children, pregnant women, or other special populations
Whether it is correlation rather than causation
```

## Output Requirements

Medical-related answers should include:

```text
Evidence level
Source date
Whether possibly outdated
Individual diagnosis requires consulting a doctor
Emergency symptoms require timely medical attention
```

## Prohibited Actions

```text
Do not use a single case to support a general conclusion
Do not use supplement marketing pages as neutral medical evidence
Do not derive diagnosis from forum experiences
Do not present correlation as causation
Do not give treatment recommendations beyond the scope of evidence
```
