# Law and Policy Information Special Rules

Legal, regulatory, policy, and compliance issues must prioritize original texts.

## Best Sources

```text
Legal provisions
Original regulations
Government official websites
Regulatory agency announcements
Court judgments
Official FAQ
Formal consultation drafts
Standard texts
```

## Secondary Sources

```text
Law firm interpretations
News reports
Policy commentary
Expert articles
Think tank analysis
```

Secondary sources are suitable for explaining context, but cannot substitute for official original texts.

## Must Check

```text
Jurisdiction
Effective date
Revision date
Whether still in effect
Whether just a draft
Whether just a consultation draft
Whether there is a transition period
Whether there are exception clauses
```

## Common Misjudgments

```text
Draft ≠ Effective law
Media interpretation ≠ Legal provision
Local policy ≠ National policy
Old regulation ≠ Currently effective regulation
Penalty case ≠ General rule
```

## Safety Note
